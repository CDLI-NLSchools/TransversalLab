Transversal Lab

Math 2201, Unit 8: Properties of Angles and Triangles (Indicators 5.1, 5.2, 5.9)

This is practice: explore two lines cut by a transversal, name angle pairs, compute measures with the reason attached, and judge whether lines must be parallel. Nothing is recorded and nothing leaves this page. One condition runs through everything here: the equalities and the supplementary pairs belong to parallel lines. The moment the lines stop being parallel, the properties stop applying.

Explore: measure cases, then break it

Drag the top of the transversal to change its slant, and drag the right end of line b to tilt it. The eight angle measures update as you move. With the parallel lock on, line b cannot tilt: measure cases, look for pairs that stay equal and pairs that stay supplementary, and record cases below. Then turn the lock off and break it: tilt line b out of parallel and watch which relationships stop holding. That failure is the evidence for indicator 5.2.
